Questions people ask us.

Most of them are about money, which is fair. If yours isn't here, email us and we'll answer it.

Getting started

What is Figures?

Figures is a digital marketing agency in Los Angeles for creators with something to sell. We run paid ads, handle creative direction, design brands and websites, help create content, manage social accounts and report on what all of it produced.

Who do you work with?

We work with creators who already have an audience and something that audience can buy. That covers people selling their own products, affiliate marketers, creators with subscriptions or memberships, and service providers like coaches.

Do you only work with creators?

Mostly, yes. The exceptions are creative direction, shoot setup, and branding and websites. We also do those for brands and businesses in other industries when they need it.

Who have you worked with?

We've worked with creators with more than 15 million followers across platforms, and with brands generating more than $100,000 a month. We keep client names off the site. Ask us on the call and we'll tell you what we did and how it went.

Do I need a certain number of followers?

No. Follower count matters less than whether people buy from you. A small audience that buys is a better fit than a big one that doesn't.

What if I don't sell anything yet?

Then ads don't make sense yet, because there's nothing to sell. If a launch is close, apply anyway and tell us when. If it's further off, we'll tell you what we'd build first.

What happens after I apply?

We read every application ourselves and reply by email. If it looks like a fit, the next step is a 15-minute call where we go through what you sell and what you need.

Money

How do you get paid?

It depends on what you sell and what you need, so there isn't one price list. On ads, it can be a share of the sales the ads generate, a fee based on your ad spend, a flat monthly fee, or a mix. Content and social media usually run on a flat monthly fee. Branding and websites are priced per project. You see all of it in writing before you agree to anything.

What does "paid on results" actually mean?

It means we want what we earn to move with what you sell. On ads, we set the deal up that way wherever it fits your business. On everything else, it means we measure the work by what you sold, and that's the number we expect to be judged on.

Is Figures a performance-based agency?

We can be, and on ads we often are. You might hear it called performance-based, revenue share or commission-based marketing, where our fee is a share of the sales the ads generate. Whether that's the right setup for you depends on what you sell, and we work it out on the call.

Who pays for the ad spend?

You do, directly to Meta, Google or TikTok from your own card. We never hold your ad budget.

How do you decide which sales came from the ads?

We agree on that in writing before anything launches. It's usually a mix of the platform's tracking and your own store or checkout data, and we both read the same number.

Is there a minimum ad budget?

It depends on what you sell and what it costs. If the budget is too small to learn anything from, we'll tell you that on the call instead of taking the work.

Do you guarantee results?

No, and you should be careful with anyone who does. What we can promise is that you'll see exactly what the work brought in.

Working together

Which platforms do you run ads on?

Meta, which covers Instagram and Facebook, Google, which covers Search and YouTube, and TikTok. Where we start depends on where your buyers are.

Can I hire you for just one thing?

Yes. Most people start with ads and add content or social later. A brand or website package also works on its own, and so does the plan.

What if I don't have a logo or a website yet?

That's what the branding and website packages are for. We can start from nothing, or rework what you already have if you're not happy with it.

What comes in a branding package?

You get three logo directions to choose from, the full logo set, your colors and typefaces, a brand guidelines document, and a brand kit with every file sized and ready to use. A website can be added to the package or done on its own.

Do I have to be in Los Angeles?

No. Most content is filmed by you, wherever you are, and we plan it with you. When we shoot it ourselves, that happens in Los Angeles. Everything else works from anywhere.

Who is behind Figures?

Figures is a division of Scheibe Advisory LLC, a strategic advisory firm in Los Angeles.
